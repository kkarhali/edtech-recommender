# Chapter 5 figure captions (verbatim, for the Writer)

Both figures were rendered directly from the per-presentation run artifacts, not from
`results/summary.md`'s prose. Rendering used a dependency-free PNG writer rather than matplotlib,
because matplotlib is not installed in `.venv-pilot` and installing it would change the
`library_versions` block recorded in `results/run_receipts/u11_s5_run_main.json`; no package was
added to the environment and no artifact under `results/` was touched.

---

**Figure 5.1. Hybrid Precision@10 relative gain over each baseline on warm users, one point per
module presentation (n = 22).**

Dataset: OULAD (Open University Learning Analytics Dataset), 32,593 learner rows counted in
`studentInfo.csv`. Metric: Precision@10 (per-presentation macro mean over evaluated learners),
K = 10. Slice: warm-user held-out slice of the primary time-based split (artifact cell key
`warm|None`), cut per module presentation by the OULAD `date` column, evaluated learners filtered
to at least 5 distinct items and at least 3 active days. Counts: 22 module presentations across 7
modules, 27,212 evaluated learners in total (375 to 2,131 per presentation). Source:
`results/run_artifacts/u11_s5_run_main/results_<pres>.json`
(`splits.primary.cells["warm|None"].models.<model>["P@10"].mean`); tabulated in
`thesis/tables/table_5_1_warm_slice.md`. Blue squares are the gain over content-based filtering,
orange diamonds the gain over item-item collaborative filtering; the dashed coloured lines mark
each series' median, the solid black line marks no difference, and the dashed red line marks C1's
pre-registered +10% threshold.

*What the figure shows.* The spread is wide, not tight. Against CBF the per-presentation gain runs
from -37.17% (GGG_2014B) to +81.90% (DDD_2013B), 16 wins and 6 losses, IQR 55.28 percentage
points, median +31.20%. Against ItemKNN-CF it runs from -26.10% (EEE_2013J) to +112.07%
(GGG_2014J), 17 wins and 5 losses, IQR 20.82 percentage points, median +10.68%. The orange median
line sits almost on top of the red threshold line: that visual near-coincidence is the 0.68
percentage-point margin by which C1's vs.-CF conjunct passes. C1 is met (both Holm-corrected exact
Wilcoxon p-values below 0.05: 0.011857 and 0.014095), but the pre-registered Wilcoxon and the
companion sign test disagree on the vs.-CBF comparison (p = 0.005929 against p = 0.052479), which
belongs in the limitations discussion. Population caveat: the warm learners evaluated here are drawn
from a highly active population (in EEE_2014J the 1,097 **log-present** learners hold a median of 49
distinct resources of a 115-item catalogue, mean 44.8, counted from `studentVle.csv`;
`methodology/threats_to_validity.md` T16), so "warm user" here means a very active learner. The
median and mean quoted are the log-present cohort's, not the evaluated cohort's.

**Figure 5.2. Hybrid Precision@10 relative gain over each baseline on cold-start-refit learners,
one point per module presentation (n = 22).**

Dataset: OULAD, as above. Metric: Precision@10, K = 10. Slice: cold-start-refit condition at k = 2
visible items (artifact cell key `A|2`), in which the item-item similarity model is refitted with
the held-out learners' rows removed from the matrix itself rather than hidden at scoring time; per
the human's Q1 ruling this refit condition is primary and decides C2 and C3. Counts: 22 module
presentations across 7 modules, 5,444 evaluated cold-start learners in total (69 to 426 per
presentation). Source: `results/run_artifacts/u11_s5_run_main/results_<pres>.json`
(`splits.primary.cells["A|2"].models.<model>["P@10"].mean`); tabulated in
`thesis/tables/table_5_2_coldstart_refit_slice.md`. Markers as in Figure 5.1; here the solid black
zero line *is* C2's threshold, since C2 requires a positive median against both baselines.

*What the figure shows.* The two series separate almost completely and in opposite directions.
Blue squares (vs. CBF) fall left of zero in 20 of 22 presentations, median -30.28%, range -73.46%
(FFF_2013B) to +71.95% (DDD_2013B). Orange diamonds (vs. CF) fall right of zero in 17 of 22, sit on
it in 3 (ties: EEE_2013J exactly; EEE_2014J and FFF_2014B equal up to floating-point summation
order, |difference| < 1e-12) and fall left of it in 2 (CCC_2014J, FFF_2013J), median +7.32%, range
-5.64% (CCC_2014J) to +173.68% (AAA_2013J). *Correction E2 (`decisions.log` 2026-09-26): an
earlier version of this caption and of the figure gave 18 of 22 with one tie and three losses,
counting EEE_2014J's +2.0e-16 as a win and FFF_2014B's as a loss; the median and ranges are
unaffected. The figure is regenerated by `thesis/figures/make_figure_5_2.py`.*
This is a consistent pattern across all 7 modules, not a few outliers, and it is the exact inverse
of the unreceipted phase 1 pilot, which beat CBF on cold start in 22 of 22 presentations by a
median +73.12% (range +13.55% to +163.85%) and lost to CF on cold start in 22 of 22 by a median
-17.90% (range -29.12% to -10.03%), recomputed per presentation from
`inputs/prior_evidence/all_modules_metrics.json#per_module_presentation[].cold_start`. *Correction
(integrity violation E1, `decisions.log` 2026-09-24): an earlier version of this caption gave the
pilot's cold-start gain over CBF as "median +190%". That figure is the pilot's **warm** gain over
CBF (median +189.54%, range +60.04% to +464.55%, the file's own
`aggregate.hybrid_vs_cbf_relative_gain_warm` block) and must not be attached to a cold-start
comparison; the 22-of-22 counts and the direction of the inversion are unaffected.* C2 is therefore
not met: the
"both baselines" requirement fails on the threshold itself, not on significance (both comparisons
are Holm-corrected significant at p_holm = 0.000241). C3 fails through the same conjunct: `ge_cbf`
holds in only 2 of 22 presentations (CCC_2014J, DDD_2013B), while `gt_mostpop`, zero empty top-10
lists and coverage@10 >= 10% hold in 22 of 22. Absolute levels are low by construction: cold-start
Precision@10 of 0.03 to 0.08 is read against a per-presentation `ceiling_p10` of 0.648 to 0.943.

**Figure 5.3. Fitted blend weight alpha on the cold-start-refit cell, per module presentation
(n = 22 presentations, 5,444 test-scored learner records).**

Dataset: OULAD, as above. Slice: cold-start-refit cell `A|2` (k = 2 visible items), the cell that
decides C2 and C3. Quantity: the per-learner blend weight the production `Hybrid` actually used
when scoring that cell, captured from `Hybrid.alpha_`. In `models.Hybrid.score` the score is
`alpha * CF + (1 - alpha) * CBF`, so **alpha is the weight on the collaborative component** and
`1 - alpha` the weight on the content component. Counts: 22 module presentations across 7 modules,
5,444 alpha records in total, equal to each cell's `n_scored` in 22 of 22 presentations (69 to 426
per presentation). Source:
`results/run_artifacts/u13d_diag_run/alpha_log_<pres>.jsonl`, receipt
`results/run_receipts/u13d_diag_run.json`; tabulated in `thesis/tables/table_5_4_diagnostics.md`.
Box spans Q1 to Q3, the heavy tick is the median, whiskers run min to max; the dashed red lines
mark 0.1 and 0.9 and the solid black line marks an even 0.5 mixture.

*Post-hoc, decides no criterion.* This diagnostic scores the test split a second time for the
production `Hybrid` outside the L6 counter (reviewer warning W3, `reviews/diag_code_review.md`).
It may not revise any criterion, threshold, model or the frozen protocol (rules 3 and 8), and
chapters 3 and 5 must label it post-hoc. The addition was verified inert: the production `Hybrid`'s
metrics in the ablation files match the receipted main run in all 44 cells, 0 differences.

*What the figure shows.* `plan.md`'s Risks section pre-registered the question of whether the
learned blend weight converges on alpha close to 1, which would mean the content and behavioural
components carry no usable marginal signal. It does not. Pooled, the weight has median 0.5150,
quartiles 0.4019 and 0.6181, range 0.0696 to 0.9651; only 0.04% of learners sit at or above 0.9
(2 of 5,444) and 0.11% at or below 0.1 (6), while 83.9% sit between 0.3 and 0.7. Per-presentation
medians run from 0.2415 (AAA_2013J) to 0.6534 (BBB_2013B), and 12 of 22 sit above 0.5. The hybrid
is a genuine, roughly even mixture on cold start, not a disguised single model.

*What it does not show.* It does not explain C2. The reading it supports is descriptive: content-
based filtering is the stronger component on the cold-start slice in 20 of 22 presentations, yet
across exactly those 20 the per-presentation median alpha, the weight on the weaker collaborative
component, runs 0.241 to 0.653 with a median of 0.494; consistently with that, the hybrid's
cold-start Precision@10 lies between its own two components in 19 of 22 presentations (above both
in 1, below both in 2), against above both in 11 of 22 and below both in 0 on the warm slice. This
is a correlate and a reading, **not a confirmed mechanism** - confirming it would mean manipulating
alpha and re-measuring, which rules 3 and 8 forbid on a closed criterion.

**Where the criteria test less than the hypothesis claims.** `idea.md`'s frozen hypothesis claims the hybrid is more accurate and more robust than either baseline alone **and** specifically outperforms them on cold-start learners. C1 tests the warm half and it holds. C2 and C3 test the cold-start half against both baselines and it does not: the hybrid beats collaborative filtering on cold start but loses to content-based filtering. No criterion tests the hypothesis's behavioural-signal clause on its own terms, because OULAD records no dwell time and the signal is operationalised as a click-volume / active-day / day-ordered-sequence proxy (recorded scope reduction, human decision Q2); no ablation isolating a behavioural recommender component was part of the frozen plan or was run, so that clause is **unchecked**, never passed (rule 6). The later D2 diagnostic (Table 5.4) removes three behavioural columns from the *blend's own feature set*, not a behavioural recommender component, so it does not test this clause either way.

**What changed relative to the phase 1 pilot, and why the pilot's reading was wrong.** The unreceipted phase 1 pilot beat CBF on cold start in 22 of 22 presentations by a median **+73.12%** (range +13.55% to +163.85%) and lost to CF on cold start in 22 of 22 (median -17.90%, range -29.12% to -10.03%), recomputed here per presentation from `inputs/prior_evidence/all_modules_metrics.json#per_module_presentation[].cold_start`. **Correction (integrity violation E1, `decisions.log` 2026-09-24):** an earlier version of this table attached "median +190%, range +60% to +465%" to the pilot's cold-start comparison. That figure is the pilot's **warm** hybrid-vs-CBF gain (median +189.54%, range +60.04% to +464.55%, the file's own `aggregate.hybrid_vs_cbf_relative_gain_warm` block), not its cold-start one; it was carried forward from the pilot's summary prose without checking which slice it belonged to. The 22-of-22 counts, the direction of every comparison and the inversion against the refit result are unaffected. The corrected design inverts both directions exactly. The named mechanism is the cold-start condition itself: the pilot hid the held-out learners at scoring time while leaving them inside the fitted similarity matrix, which inflated the collaborative component for learners who were supposed to be new and starved the content component of any comparative advantage. Refitting the matrix without those learners (Module A, human decision Q1) removes that leak. This mechanism is **confirmed** in the sense that the pilot's numbers were reproduced bit-identically under C6 and the corrected condition is the only deliberate change to how the cold-start slice is built; it is **not** separately ablated, so the attribution rests on the design change plus the reproduction, not on a controlled A/B of the two conditions.

**Most likely cause of the C2 / C3 shortfall (idea.md criterion 4).** The explanation offered in the first analysis cycle -- that the blend classifier's supervision collapses on cold start (all-zero-label drop rate median 43.97% against 7.69% on warm, positive-label share median 50.95% against 80.60%, from `results_<pres>.json#splits.primary.cells["A|2"].info.blend`) -- has since been tested as far as the files on disk allow, and is **withdrawn**: across the 22 presentations that drop share correlates *positively* with the hybrid's cold-start gain over CBF (Spearman rho +0.434, p = 0.0435, n = 22, exploratory and uncorrected), the opposite sign to what the explanation predicts. What the evidence now supports is weaker and descriptive. Diagnostic D1 shows the fitted blend weight does not converge near 1 (pooled median 0.515 over 5,444 cold-start learner records, quartiles 0.402 and 0.618, 0.04% at or above 0.9), so on cold start the blend places roughly half its weight on the collaborative component although content-based filtering is the stronger component there in 20 of 22 presentations, and the hybrid consequently lands between its own two components in 19 of 22 -- a roughly even mixture of a strong and a weak ranker sits between them, which is where C2 and C3 find it. This is a **correlate and a reading, not a confirmed mechanism**, and it rests on post-hoc diagnostics that may not revise any criterion, threshold or model (rules 3 and 8).
